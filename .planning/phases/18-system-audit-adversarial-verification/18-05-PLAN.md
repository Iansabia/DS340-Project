<objective>
Tier 4 audit: confirm the pair universe used to train and test the headline models was constructed without survivorship bias. Three sub-audits in one script:

(a) **Candidate universe construction** — every pair_id ever considered (from `data/processed/aligned_pairs.parquet` + `data/live/active_matches.json`).
(b) **Realized universe** — pairs actually in `data/processed/test.parquet`.
(c) **Drop-reason classification + random spot-check** — for the dropped set, classify each via heuristic (low_overlap if < 20 bars; pre_test_window if max_ts before split; REVIEW otherwise). Random-sample 10 with seed=42 for manual classification (Ian must sign off per VALIDATION.md manual-only verifications row).

Per RESEARCH.md, Phase 14 already disclosed survivorship bias in §6.4 item 3; this audit's job is to **quantify** the survivorship rate (drop_rate %) and produce a 10-pair manual sample with reasons, NOT to acknowledge it abstractly. Strongest possible evidence: "drop rate of X% with structural reasons in all 10 random samples."

Output: `experiments/audit/audit_survivorship.py` + passing fixture test + `survivorship_audit.json` containing candidate count, realized count, dropped count, drop_rate, 10-pair random_sample with classifications, and assumption stack.

Implements requirement: AUDIT-04.
</objective>
