<objective>
Resolve the leakage finding from Tier 2 (Plan 18-03) by retraining the headline models on a pair-stratified train/test split where no pair bridges the boundary, then recomputing Sharpe under the same Tier 1 protocol used in Plan 18-02. Produces the leakage-free numbers that Plan 18-07's paper updates will use as the new headline (replacing the un-traceable 3.2 in PAPER_DRAFT.md / slides_deck.html).

This plan was added 2026-04-26 after Ian rejected Option A (replace 3.2 with 7.04) at the 18-07 checkpoint. Rationale: the BLdP-corrected 7.04 was computed on the same leaky data the embargo audit failed; swapping un-validated numbers is not the kill-or-confirm answer. The honest answer is to rebuild the split, retrain, and let the chips fall.

Scope is tight: LR + XGBoost only (these are the headline models per Phase 17). No GRU/LSTM/TFT/PPO retraining — those are not load-bearing for the leadline number. If the purged Sharpe drops materially, that becomes the new paper headline; the methodology section gains an explicit "we re-ran with pair-stratified split because the audit found embargo violations in the original canonical split" paragraph.

Implements requirement: AUDIT-07.
</objective>
